Grimes are delighted to introduce ‘Carriglea', No. 2 Harbour Road, Skerries, a remarkable four-bedroom period townhouse enjoying a truly exceptional coastal setting.

Dating from circa 1890, this substantial home presents an exciting opportunity to restore and modernise a property of genuine character, with the rare benefit of direct access from the rear garden onto North Beach.

Internally, the property retains many of its original period features, including generous ceiling heights, ornate coving, original timber shutters, picture rails and open fireplaces, providing an excellent foundation for creating a distinctive and character-filled home.

The property has been vacant for over two years and may qualify for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant, subject to the relevant eligibility criteria, potentially providing valuable financial assistance towards the refurbishment works.

Ideally positioned on the Harbour Road in the heart of Skerries and within easy reach of the town's excellent range of cafés, restaurants, bars, boutiques, supermarkets, schools, sports clubs and excellent transport links, with both the harbour and North Beach immediately accessible.

A rare opportunity to acquire a substantial period home in one of Skerries' most sought-after coastal locations, with enormous potential to create a truly special residence. Viewing is highly recommended.
